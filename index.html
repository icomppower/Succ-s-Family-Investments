Founded in 1998, Succès Family Investments is a privately held real estate platform stewarding capital on behalf of the founding family, select institutional partners, and a small circle of multi-generational families. Our edge is patience: we underwrite to a decade, not a quarter, and we own only what we would be content to hold indefinitely.

We seek durable irreplaceability — assets and submarkets where supply is constrained, demand is structural, and operating excellence compounds value over time. Every dollar of capital is treated as if it were the family's own, because most of it is.

We are buyers of land, builders of place, and stewards of capital — in that order, and for that long.
Émile Succès · Founder & Chair
Strategy

Three principles, applied with discipline.

Our investment approach has remained essentially unchanged for over two decades. We believe that durable outperformance comes from doing a small number of things exceptionally well, repeatedly, across cycles.

01 PILLAR 01

Value Acquisition

We source off-market and complex situations where price reflects friction, not fundamentals — well-located assets, sponsor recapitalizations, and family successions where speed and certainty are valued above marginal price.

  • Direct sponsor relationships
  • Discretionary capital, fast close
  • Complex situations & recaps
02 PILLAR 02

Active Asset Management

Ownership is where returns are made. We operate every asset in-house with dedicated leasing, capital projects, and ESG teams — a vertically integrated platform built to extract durable NOI growth from quality real estate.

  • In-house operating platform
  • Tenant retention & experience
  • Programmatic repositioning
03 PILLAR 03

Capital Preservation

Conservative leverage, laddered maturities, and a deep balance sheet allow us to be patient sellers and opportunistic buyers — particularly in the dislocations that define every cycle. Downside discipline drives upside return.

  • Fixed-rate, long-duration debt
  • Cycle-tested underwriting
  • Family-aligned governance
